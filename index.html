About Me

Hello! I'm Diganta Das, an engineer driven by curiosity, a passion for technology and a desire to explore new possibilities.

With an academic background in Electronics and Communication Engineering and Data Analytics, my journey has taken me across different areas of technology, including embedded systems, machine learning and data-driven problem-solving.

Curious by nature, adventurous in spirit and occasionally sarcastic, I believe there's always something new to learn and somewhere new to explore.
